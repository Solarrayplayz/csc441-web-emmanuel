Projects

Here are some of the projects I've worked on while at Le Moyne College & in my free time.

Loki Tennis Drill Book

An animated playbook of six on-court drills built for an aggressive counterpuncher: a player who defends with movement and attacks short balls. Each drill is a top-down court animation with play/pause, speed control and a scrubber, plus coaching cues and a training dose. It's an interactive web page built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript canvas animation. One thing that was hard getting the timing and movement to look right.

Block Palace

A pixel-style web app for training your memory with the memory palace technique. It opens with a tutorial, lets you build your own route of stops, and has six game modes, including Speed Cards, Shopping Sprint and Names and Faces. Your progress saves in the browser. Designing what the game would look like was the hardest part.
